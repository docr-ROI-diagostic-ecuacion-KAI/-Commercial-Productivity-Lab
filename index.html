METHODOLOGY AT THE END OF THE JOURNEY

DIIIP explains how qualified demand becomes executable commercial evidence

SQL creates value when upstream demand becomes a traceable assisted-sale decision, a measurable use of commercial time and a clean handoff to Sales without collecting banking credentials.

D
Data

MQL, Loyalty, Call Center and salesperson-created leads; customer/SPO context; Product Page; Procurement Price Policy; offer conditions; quantity; elapsed commercial time and outcome.

I
Information

The SQL record structures lead origin, opportunity owner, customer status, SPO, quantity, commercial condition, final price, elapsed time and conversion status.

I
Intelligence

Qualification rules, authorised price-policy limits, assisted PPOS logic and the productivity baremo transform commercial evidence into controlled action.

I
Insights

The dashboard reveals source conversion productivity, salesperson productivity, standard versus actual time, commercial resource cost and cost per conversion order.

P
Personalization Actions

The salesperson adapts the assisted offer to the customer context, creates the Direct Conversion Order, prepares the Thank-you Email and hands the approved commercial evidence to Sales.
