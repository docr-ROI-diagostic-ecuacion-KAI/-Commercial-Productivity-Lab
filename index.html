    03 · Commercial Productivity

    Measure standard work completed against actual hours. Inspect the process before judging the person: task-level evidence reveals where time, tools or capability need attention.

    P · PRODUCTIVITY
    Reporting viewStart with the total team, then inspect one adviser when needed.

    Log manual activity

    How P is calculated

    1 point = 1 standard hour. Each completed task earns its standard points. Productivity for a period is points divided by actual hours consumed.

    P is an operating measure. It must be read with quality and customer outcomes; it is not a standalone people decision.
